/ changes to ax since initial release

unreleased
New sub-module kx.ax.qdocparser (exported from kx.ax as qdp): a pure-q qdoc parser with no dependencies.
qdoc reads q source through it: qd.doc, getFiles and the tags call give the same results, and qd.doc is
many times faster (about 36x on a 2,000-item corpus). qdoc's parse no longer uses PCRE2; its renderer still does.

2026.04.27
qp.go now sends to stdout sixel encoding of image (previously returned error). requires fixes present in KDB-X 2026.04.10
